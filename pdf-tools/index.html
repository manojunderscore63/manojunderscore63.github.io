About the app

One toolkit for
all your documents.

PDF Tools brings together everything you normally need a desktop for — compressing, merging, splitting and rearranging PDF files — into one clean, easy Android app.

Scan paper documents with your camera, convert images and text into PDFs, pull pages back out as images, and print straight to a wireless printer. Whatever the document, it takes just a few taps.
